Frequently Asked Questions

Clear facts about rooftop solar, WBSEDCL net-metering, and government subsidies.

What is M.T. Solar by MTC?

M.T. Solar by MTC is the specialized rooftop solar business by M. T. Construction, an established company in West Bengal with approximately 25 years of electrical, mechanical, and government contracting experience, including enlistment with WBSEDCL and WBSETCL. We deliver turnkey on-grid, off-grid, and hybrid solar systems.

How much subsidy do I receive under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

Under the PM Surya Ghar national scheme:
  • 1 kW residential system: ₹30,000 Central DBT subsidy
  • 2 kW residential system: ₹60,000 Central DBT subsidy
  • 3 kW and higher residential systems: ₹78,000 Central DBT subsidy (maximum cap)
Additional State Government Subsidy for SC/ST Beneficiaries: In West Bengal, eligible Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) domestic electricity consumers qualify for additional State Government rooftop solar subsidies and tribal welfare clean energy grants, further reducing the out-of-pocket investment. Our team assists with complete documentation and portal verification for both Central and State SC/ST benefit approvals.

We manage the entire application and documentation directly on the national portal on your behalf.

How does WBSEDCL net-metering work?

A bidirectional net meter replaces your traditional meter. During sunny hours, excess solar energy generated by your panels flows back into the WBSEDCL grid, turning your meter backward. You are billed only for the net units consumed, dramatically slashing electricity bills by up to 80–90%.

Are the solar structures safe during coastal cyclones and high winds?

Yes. South 24 Parganas is prone to severe coastal storms. We utilize heavy-gauge hot-dip galvanized mounting structures with structural bracing anchored firmly into RCC roof slabs via chemical fasteners. Structures are engineered to resist wind speeds exceeding 160 kmph.

Which solar panel and inverter brands are used?

We evaluate and install premier Tier-1 ALMM-listed Indian module manufacturers evaluated from premier Tier-1 ALMM-listed Indian module manufacturers, paired with high-efficiency grid-tied inverters from leading global manufacturers with IP65 weatherproofing and 5–10 year warranties.
