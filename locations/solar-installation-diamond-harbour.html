Are solar panel structures in Diamond Harbour safe during coastal cyclones?

How does WBSEDCL net-metering work in Diamond Harbour?

After the solar system is installed, a joint inspection is conducted with the local WBSEDCL Diamond Harbour Customer Care Center. A bidirectional net meter is installed that records units exported to the grid and units imported, billing you only for the net difference.
