Cell Technology

Mono-PERC vs TOPCon Solar Modules: What Works Best in Bengal?

Engineering Comparison • Performance under Tropical Humidity & Heat

The photovoltaic industry has evolved rapidly from older polycrystalline blue cells to modern high-efficiency Monocrystalline PERC (Passivated Emitter and Rear Cell) and newer N-Type TOPCon (Tunnel Oxide Passivated Contact) modules. Understanding the difference helps you make the right long-term investment.

Why Temperature Coefficient Matters in West Bengal

During summer months in South 24 Parganas, rooftop surface temperatures often climb past 50°C. Standard solar panels lose efficiency as temperatures climb:

  • Mono-PERC: Typical temperature coefficient of -0.35%/°C. Power loss occurs during peak midday heat.
  • TOPCon N-Type: Superior temperature coefficient of -0.30%/°C. Retains higher output on scorching hot afternoons.

Bifacial Generation on Flat RCC Terraces

Many TOPCon modules feature bifacial glass-to-glass architecture. In Bengal, where concrete terraces are often painted with reflective white cooling coatings, bifacial panels capture scattered albedo light from the roof underneath, generating an additional 5% to 15% extra units of power.
