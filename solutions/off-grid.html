Why Choose an Off-Grid System?

Continuous electricity day and night, independent of utility power cuts.

Tubular & Lithium Storage

High-discharge deep-cycle C10 tubular solar batteries or long-life Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery banks tailored to your nighttime energy demand.

MPPT Solar Charge Controller

Advanced Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) extracts up to 30% more power from solar modules on cloudy delta days to charge battery banks rapidly.

Zero Blackout Anxiety

Automatic microsecond switchover keeps your critical refrigeration, water pumps, lighting, and communication devices active during seasonal power disruptions.
