How Grid-Tied Solar Functions

Zero battery replacement costs and effortless automatic synchronization.

1. Generation

High-efficiency solar panels capture daylight and generate Direct Current (DC) power.

2. Inversion

Grid-tied string inverter converts DC to 230V/415V Alternating Current (AC) for appliances.

3. Self-Consumption

Your home directly utilizes solar electricity first, drastically reducing grid import.

4. Net-Metering

Extra power flows to the WBSEDCL grid. Your meter reverses and banking credits accrue.
