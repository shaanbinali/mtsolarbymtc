How to Apply & Claim Subsidy (Step-by-Step)

1
Portal Registration & WBSEDCL Linking

Register on the National Portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) by selecting West Bengal • WBSEDCL and entering your 9-digit Consumer ID.

2
Technical Feasibility Approval (TFA)

We coordinate the Technical Feasibility Approval (TFA) with WBSEDCL for distribution transformer capacity allocation.

3
Vendor Selection: Select M. T. Construction

Under vendor selection on the PM Surya Ghar portal, choose M. T. Construction as your empaneled vendor name. Our team will verify the selection, upload system agreements, and dispatch local engineering teams.

4
Installation & Net-Meter Sync

Our engineers install approved ALMM solar modules, test chemical earthing, and coordinate bi-directional net-meter replacement with WBSEDCL.

5
Commissioning & Direct Bank Subsidy (DBT)

We submit the joint commissioning certificate. The central subsidy (up to ₹78,000) is credited straight into your DBT-linked bank account within 30 days.

Required Documents Checklist

  • Recent WBSEDCL Electricity Bill (Domestic)
  • Aadhaar Card of Electricity Consumer
  • Cancelled Cheque / Bank Passbook Copy
  • Photograph of Shadow-Free Rooftop
  • Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar & WBSEDCL
